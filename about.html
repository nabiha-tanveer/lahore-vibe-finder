About VibeFinder

Why we built this, and who it's for.

"Finding the right spot shouldn't take longer than actually enjoying it."

Our Story

Lahore is full of hidden cafés, quiet study corners, and rooftop hangouts — but finding the right one for your mood was always a matter of luck or word-of-mouth. VibeFinder was built to change that: a simple, mood-first way to discover spots across the city that actually match what you're looking for, whether it's a quiet place to study before an exam or a chill café to unwind with friends.

Our Mission

To help students and locals in Lahore find spots that fit their vibe, budget, and schedule — without endless scrolling through generic review sites. Every spot on VibeFinder is tagged by mood, rated by real visitors, and bookable in a few clicks.

🎓

Built for Students

Designed with PUCIT and other Lahore students in mind — budget-friendly options and quiet study spots are front and center.

🏙️

Local Focus

Every spot listed is based in Lahore — from DHA to Gulberg to roadside chai stalls near campus.

🛠️

Built With

A fully static site — HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No backend, no frameworks, no database.
